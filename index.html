Two teams of two from early March 2027. Tranche 1: 100 stations live by June (target: 100 scanned by end of May). Tranche 2: all 250 live by October. About 320 team-days are available, so routes must cluster.

Assumptions (used by route and programme)
Defaults from the cost model: £180 day rate, £30 subsistence, £100 hotel per person per night, £30 fuel per van-day, £15 parking per station day. Small stations take 1 day and cluster stations half a day; large and medium are set to 2 and 1.25 days so the 250 fit the model's 320 team-days (the model allows up to 3 and 2). Drive times use straight-line distance × road factor at an average speed; check each day in Google Maps. These settings are kept in your browser only.
